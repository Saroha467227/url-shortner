Where does my request go?
  1. Express checks your IP's token bucket and validates your URL.
  2. PostgreSQL saves the short code, destination, and expiry.
  3. Redis remembers the destination after its first lookup for up to five minutes.
  4. For each accepted visit, PostgreSQL adds one click. Express sends a 302 response and your browser opens the destination.
